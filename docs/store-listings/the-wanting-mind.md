## 4. ASO keyword research

No paid ASO tool or volume data was available, so the volume and competition columns are **estimates**. The rules below come from current guides: [applaunchflow](https://www.applaunchflow.com/blog/app-store-keyword-field-guide-2026), [appdrift](https://appdrift.co/blog/ios-app-store-keywords-field), [adapty JP](https://adapty.io/blog/markdown/how-to-optimize-aso-for-japan.md), [appsops locale primer](https://appsops.store/blog/app-store-keywords-by-locale-aso-primer).
- Apple indexes name + subtitle + keywords and combines them across fields, so a word in one field is wasted in another.
- Keywords take commas with no spaces, singular forms, and no "app" or "the".
- In Japan, split compound words into separate tokens, and use kanji or katakana rather than romaji.
- Play has no keyword field. It indexes the title, short description and full description, so natural repetition in the full description matters there.

| Locale | Keyword | Why | Est. volume / competition | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| en-US | philosophy | Core genre; high-intent books search | High / medium | subtitle (proposed) |
| en-US | desire | Literally the book's subject; low competition | Low-med / low | subtitle (proposed) |
| en-US | audiobook | Real feature (opening chapters free, full audiobook via IAP) | High / high | keywords |
| en-US | psychology | Overlaps "why we want"; broad interest | High / high | keywords |
| en-US | economics, neuroscience | Two of the four lenses the book uses | Med / low-med | keywords |
| en-US | dopamine | Popular search; 8 chapters discuss it | Med / med | keywords |
| en-US | buddhism, capitalism, inequality | Distinct topics with low app competition | Low-med / low | keywords |
| ja | 欲望 / 哲学 / 心理学 / 経済学 | Subject + three lenses, kanji tokens | 哲学 and 心理学 med-high | subtitle (proposed) |
| ja | オーディオブック, 朗読, 英語 | Listening intent; the narration is English, which suits learners | med | keywords |

| ja | 脳科学, 神経科学, ドーパミン | 脳科学 is the everyday Japanese word for neuroscience | med | keywords |
| ja | 教養, 読書, 電子書籍 | Japanese "liberal-arts reading" intent | med | keywords |

## 5. Policy flags (mental health and wellbeing adjacency)

The book discusses wanting, craving, addiction-style "hook" design, depression statistics, cults and psychedelics (ch-16c, ch-24). The app is a **book in Books**, not a health app. The draft copy makes no "treats", "cures", "therapy", "improves wellbeing" or "reduces eye strain" claims. Warmth and 20-20-20 are described by what they do.

- **Apple 1.4.1 (physical harm, medical apps):** does not apply. No diagnosis, measurement or health data.
- **Apple 5.1.2(i) (third-party AI):** Ask the book sends passage text and the question to an LLM (Langfuse-traced). Confirm the in-app disclosure or consent and the privacy policy cover this.
- **Play Health apps declaration:** answer "no health features". Do not tick wellness or mental health.
- **Age rating (owner question, not asserted):** the live rating is 4+/Everyone. Chapter 24 discusses psilocybin, LSD and DMT, and 16c discusses cults, both informationally. Re-run Apple's age-rating questionnaire (the "Alcohol, Tobacco, or Drug Use or References" item) and the IARC questionnaire, and accept whatever rating they return.
- **Play metadata policy:** no "free", "#1" or "best" in the title (none present). "Free" in the short or full description is factual (the text is free).
- **Optional trust line** (not added to the copy; owner's call): "the wanting mind is a book of ideas, not medical, psychological or financial advice."
