Hi Product Hunt, I'm Kenji. I make small apps on my own, and this one is a book.

The question behind it: why do so many institutions feel like they take more than they give back? Economics, ethics and neuroscience each describe part of it, in their own words. The Wanting Mind puts them on one spectrum, from extraction (taking more than you return) to generation (leaving more than you took), so you can see the same pattern in a company, a friendship and a civilization.

What's in it:

1. The full text, free to read on the web with no account and no ads.
2. A 3D map of the book's concepts. Click a node and you land in the passage that explains it.
3. Interactive simulations of the book's arguments.
4. Narration on the opening chapters.
5. Editions in English, Japanese, Chinese and Thai.

To be upfront: the narration is AI-generated (ElevenLabs, with a different narrator voice per chapter), and so are the illustrations.

There's an optional supporter purchase in the apps for people who want to fund it. The book stays free either way.

What I'd like to hear: where the argument loses you, and whether the map helps you read or just looks nice.
