Hi Product Hunt, I'm Kenji. I make small apps on my own.

This one came from a familiar loop: you hand your phone to someone, get back a photo with your head cut off, and both of you go "let me see… delete it". The person who cares how the photo looks is the one who can't see the screen.

So the app splits the job across two phones:

1. One phone is the camera. The other shows its live view, phone to phone when the network allows.
2. The person in the photo taps directions (left, right, closer, back), taps a spot on the view, or sends one of 30+ pose ideas.
3. "Perfect! Take it!" and the shot lands in a private gallery you both share, with albums and a monthly recap.

Pairing takes a short code, a QR scan or an invite link. It works across iPhone, Android and the web, in English, Thai, Chinese and Japanese. Pairing and live capture are free, with no account and no ads. Some AI features use credits after the free daily ones.

About the name: it's the phrase people already search for, so it stayed. The app itself works for anyone taking a photo of anyone.

What I'd like to hear: where the live view lags on your network, and which directions you wish you could send.
