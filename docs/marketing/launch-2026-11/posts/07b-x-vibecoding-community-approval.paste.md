Hi Vibe Coding mods. I'd like approval to introduce a tool on r/vibecoding.

Mushi Mushi is open source and free for one app. It finds what's broken in a live app built with Lovable, Bolt, Cursor or Claude Code, and explains it in plain words, with a fix your AI can apply.

Two parts:
1. A free check of your live site, no sign-up: broken links and images, pages that don't load, "Lovable App" titles.
2. A report button for your users. Each report comes back as a plain-English cause plus a fix prompt.

Try it: https://kensaur.us/mushi-mushi/docs/check?src=x.vibecoding-approval
Code: https://github.com/kensaurus/mushi-mushi
