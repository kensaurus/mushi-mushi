Users don't report bugs.

I build apps with AI, and I run nine of them with real users. Each one has a "Report a problem" button.

In the 60 days to 11 November, people pressed it 34 times. In the same 60 days, 2,031 other bug reports came in from automatic checks and my own testing. Of 523 fixes merged, 2 started from a person's report.

If you built your app with Lovable, Bolt, Cursor or Claude Code, the quiet is not good news. Most people who hit a broken link, a page that won't load or a form that does nothing just close the tab.

Two free things you can do this week:

1. Check your live site. Paste the URL, no sign-up: https://kensaur.us/mushi-mushi/docs/check?src=linkedin.datastory

2. Give the people who do care a way to tell you. In Lovable or Bolt, it's one prompt. Each report comes back in plain English, with a fix your AI can apply: https://kensaur.us/mushi-mushi/docs/quickstart/lovable-bolt?src=linkedin.datastory

The tool is Mushi Mushi. It's open source, and the live numbers are here: https://kensaur.us/mushi-mushi/docs/proof?src=linkedin.datastory
