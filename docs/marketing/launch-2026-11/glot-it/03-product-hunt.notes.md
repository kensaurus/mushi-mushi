## Rules that matter

- From Product Hunt's own guide (help.producthunt.com, "Build your product
  page"): write like a person; say who you are, why you built it, what it
  does, who it's for, and ask a specific question. **"Ask for feedback, never
  for upvotes."**
- Don't ask friends, users or groups to upvote. No paid boosts.
- PH launches go live at 00:01 Pacific (17:01 JST in winter). Be there for the
  first 3 hours, then check every couple of hours until midnight Pacific.
