1/4 Thai has five tones, and a wrong one rarely gets corrected.

Say หมา (mǎa) when you mean ม้า (máa) and you've asked for a dog, not a horse. People understand from context and move on, so the mistake sticks.

2/4 I make small apps on my own. This one is for Thai learners: it plays a word, you pick the tone, and in speaking practice you record yourself and see where the tone slipped.

Words you miss come back a few days later, before you forget them.

3/4 Three pairs worth drilling:

ใกล้ glâi (near) / ไกล glai (far)
ข่าว khàao (news) / ข้าว khâao (rice)
มา maa (come) / ม้า máa (horse)
