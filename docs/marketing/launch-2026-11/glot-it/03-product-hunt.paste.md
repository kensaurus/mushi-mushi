Hi Product Hunt, I'm Kenji. I make small apps on my own, and glot.it is the one for Thai.

The problem it tackles: a wrong tone in Thai rarely gets corrected. People understand you from context, so you can say หมา (dog) when you mean ม้า (horse) for months without knowing.

What it does:

1. Drills play a word and ask which tone you heard.
2. Speaking practice records you and tells you where the tone slipped.
3. Words you miss come back a few days later, spaced so they stick.
4. The alphabet, grammar notes and 12 conversation scenarios with a voice tutor.

All 161 lessons are free. Pro lifts the daily AI limits (the free tutor gets 10 turns a day) and adds offline downloads. No ads inside a lesson.
