Hi Product Hunt, I'm Kenji. I make small apps on my own, and this one is for the conversations people put off.

The roommate who never does the dishes. Asking your manager for a raise. The apology you owe a friend. Most of us rehearse those in our heads, badly, and then say them for real once.

lets-talk gives you a practice run:

1. Advise: paste the line that froze you. See how it might look from their side, then pick from a few replies you could actually send.
2. Write: draft what you'd say and get it scored on four plain parts (what happened, how you feel, what you need, and a request they can say yes or no to). A calmer rewrite sits next to yours.
3. Role-play: talk it through with an AI character who has a mood of their own and doesn't fold on the first try. A vibe meter shows the heat, and a coach note after each turn says what landed.

It's built on Nonviolent Communication, a framework used in mediation. You can start as a guest in the browser with no account and no API key. Before anything you type leaves your device, it shows what is sent and asks for your OK. Your text goes to Anthropic's Claude, and it isn't stored on my servers.

It's a practice tool, not therapy or crisis support.

What I'd like to hear: which conversation you'd rehearse first, and where the AI character felt fake.
