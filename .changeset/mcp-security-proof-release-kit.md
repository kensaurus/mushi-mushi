Three tools for the release stage: `get_security_proof` (the scans your own CI ran for a release's commit), `export_release_kit` (a signed kit of screenshots and clips for a product-video tool) and `request_store_media_upload` (ask to put new screenshots, app previews or listing images on the App Store or Google Play; a person approves each upload in the console first).
