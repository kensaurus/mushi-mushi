# You built your app with Lovable or Bolt

## What goes wrong for you

You described your app in a chat and Lovable (or Bolt) built it. Now real
people use it, and sometimes something breaks. You can't read the code, and a
customer saying "it doesn't work" doesn't tell you where to look. Most people
who hit a bug don't even say that. They just leave.

## What Mushi Mushi does

- It adds a **"Report a problem" button** to your app. A customer taps it and
  types one sentence. Mushi Mushi adds a picture of their screen, the page they
  were on and what they tapped.
- You get a **short note in plain English**: what broke, how serious it is and
  the likely cause.
- It writes the **fix instructions** for you: the exact words to paste back
  into Lovable or Bolt.
- If you want, [check your live site free](/check), with no sign-up, to catch
  problems nobody has reported yet.

## What you do

1. Sign up free and create a project.
2. Copy the line Mushi Mushi gives you (your app's details are already in it)
   and ask Lovable or Bolt to add it. You paste a prompt; there is no terminal.
3. Publish your app. When a report comes in, read the note, paste the fix
   instructions into Lovable or Bolt, check the change and publish again.

Nothing changes in your app until you approve it.

## How long it takes

About five minutes to set up. A test report gets its plain-English diagnosis in
about 10 seconds.

## What it costs

Free for one app: 100 diagnoses a month, no card and no AI key of your own. A
diagnosis is one bug explained, and repeat reports of the same bug count once.
More apps are on [Builder and Studio](/pricing).
