# You have a phone app (Expo, React Native or Capacitor)

## What goes wrong for you

On a phone, people don't email you when something breaks. They close the app,
and sometimes they leave a one-star review a week later. By then you can't
tell which screen, which phone or which version it was.

## What Mushi Mushi does

- Your users **shake the phone**, or tap a small bug button, and type one
  sentence. The report can include a screenshot of the screen they were on.
- No signal? The report **waits on the phone** and sends itself once the phone
  is back online.
- You get a **plain-English diagnosis** (what broke, how serious it is, the
  likely cause) and a **fix prompt** for Cursor, Claude Code or another AI
  editor.

Native iOS, Android and Flutter libraries are previews.

## What you do

1. Run `npx mushi-mushi` in your app's folder. It finds Expo, React Native or
   Capacitor, installs the right package and saves your project ID and key.
2. Make a new build of your app, so the button ships with it.
3. When a report comes in, read the diagnosis, paste the fix prompt into your AI
   editor, check the change and ship an update.

## How long it takes

A few minutes for the setup, plus the time your app takes to build.
