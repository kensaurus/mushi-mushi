# To-do

**Route:** `/inbox` (sidebar: **More pages › Bugs › To-do**, or keep it in the sidebar as a Lab from Settings → Display & sidebar)

> **Scenario:** It's Monday morning. You open Mushi Mushi and want to know in one
> glance what actually needs a human decision right now, without clicking through
> Bugs, Processing jobs and Mushi health separately to piece it together.

To-do is your list across the whole loop: every action that still needs you, in one
prioritised view. It has three tabs: **Overview** (every open card, top to bottom),
**Stages** (filter by step, open and cleared cards together) and **Activity** (the
recent reports and fixes behind the counts).

---

## How it works

The cards come from `GET /v1/admin/dashboard`, and the status line and tab title from
`GET /v1/admin/inbox/stats`. The page stays open (never "All clear") while any critical
or high report is unfixed, including Sentry imports the classifier has already tagged.
Every card you see is a state in the pipeline that Mushi has identified as requiring a
human decision or awareness. A page you opened before shows its last data at once and
refreshes behind it.

---

## Reading an action card

Each card has four parts:

| Part | Example |
|------|---------|
| **PDCA stage chip** | `PLAN` (triage backlog), `DO` (failed fix), `OPS` (dead-letter item) |
| **Page label** | The page that owns the action |
| **Action title** | "2 critical reports need triage", "3 reports still unfixed after an auto-fix attempt" |
| **Reason** | One line on why it matters |

The **primary action button** takes you directly to the page with the relevant item
pre-selected. You never need to search.

---

## Cleared stages strip

Below the open cards, a row of chips shows every pipeline stage that currently has
**nothing pending**, with a ✓ badge. When all chips are checked and there are no open
cards, the page reads **All clear**.

Click any cleared chip to navigate to that page and verify the state yourself.

---

## Filtering by stage

On the **Stages** tab, the filter chips focus on one part of the loop. **All**,
**Open** and **Clear** filter by state; the stage chips appear only for stages that
have cards:

| Filter | Shows |
|--------|-------|
| **All** | Every card, open and cleared |
| **Open** | Cards that still need you |
| **Clear** | Stages with nothing pending |
| **Plan** | Classify and triage |
| **Do** | Dispatch and land fixes |
| **Check** | Verify quality |
| **Act** | Connections and config |
| **Ops** | Health and compliance |

## Common tasks

### Morning triage (the fast path)
1. Open To-do. Look at the **Open** count.
2. If it's 0, everything is cleared. Home is the next stop.
3. If not, scan the cards top to bottom; the most urgent comes first.
4. Click the primary action on each card to resolve it.
5. Click **Refresh**: cleared cards disappear as you resolve them.

### When To-do seems wrong
If you resolved an issue but the card is still showing, click **Refresh** to fetch
the counts again.

---

<Callout type="info">
To-do is a read-only view: it doesn't take actions itself. Every primary action
button navigates to the page that owns the action, so actions are logged on their
source page, not here.
</Callout>
