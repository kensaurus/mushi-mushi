# Help & support

**Route:** `/feedback` (sidebar: **Help & support**, under Account; the page was called Feedback hub before)

> **Scenario:** You filed support tickets with the Mushi Mushi team from the console and
> want one place for active vs shipped feedback with unread replies.

Tabs: **Overview · Active · Shipped · All**.

- **Overview**: a status banner, the top priority, and how support tickets differ from
  your users' bug reports.
- **Active**: open and in-progress tickets. A **New reply** badge marks an unread reply.
- **Shipped**: ideas linked to a release, with a version chip showing where your request landed.
- **All**: the full history, filterable by **Bugs** or **Features**.

Open a ticket to see its detail. Feature-category tickets with community votes live on
[Feature requests](/admin/feature-board).
