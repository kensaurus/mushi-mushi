# Triage grading

**Route:** `/judge` (sidebar: **Triage grading**, under Verify; formerly Judge)

A second AI grades each diagnosis Mushi Mushi makes: was the category right, the
severity, the part of the app, and were the steps to reproduce usable. This page shows
how often it agrees, so you can tell whether to trust a diagnosis before you merge its fix.

**First on the page:** one line that says how many diagnoses the grader disagreed with
**in the last 14 days** ("7 of 12 diagnoses in the last 14 days disagreed with the grader")
and a **Review 7** button. An older server without the window says "so far", all time.
Beside it: **Agreed** as a bar against the 80% target, the disagreements still to review,
and the reports ready to grade.

Under it, **How often the grader agrees** draws one bar per week for 12 weeks against a
dashed 80% target line (green at or over it, amber under, red under 60%). **Worst
disagreements, last 14 days** lists the 3 lowest-graded disagreements as review rows.

### Reviewing a disagreement

**Review N** opens **Grades** filtered to the last 14 days of disagreements, rows still to review first, one compact row each: the report,
its grade as a bar, the grader's reason in one line, and the decision:

- **Accept grader's view** applies the grader's correction to the report (category,
  severity or area; only values the report accepts are offered).
- **Keep diagnosis** keeps the report as it is and adds a team note.

Both confirm the report's category as a person, so the row turns into **✓ Reviewed** and
leaves the count. Confirming a category also lets a reporter's feature request go to
auto-fix. A row with no correction to offer, a report filed under a reporter's own category
(feature, question, feedback), or a server older than this page, shows
**Open report** instead. Viewers can read the rows but not decide.

`GET /v1/admin/judge/stats` returns `recentWindowDays`, `recentEvaluations`,
`recentDisagreements`, `recentToReview` and `agreementWeeks`; each row of
`GET /v1/admin/judge/evaluations` carries `grader_patch` and `reviewed`.

## Tabs

The page has four tabs: **Overview**, **Trend**, **Grades** and **Prompts**.

- **Trend**: **Score trend (12w)** with a bar per dimension (**Accuracy**, **Severity**,
  **Component**, **Repro**), a **Score distribution**, and a **Weekly history** table
  with the week, each dimension and the number of evaluations.
- **Grades**: **Recent evaluations**, one row per graded report with the model, the
  prompt version, each dimension and whether the grader **Agreed**. Tick
  **Disagreements only** to see the misses.
- **Prompts**: the **Prompt leaderboard** with stage, version, status (`active` or
  `candidate`), score, evaluations and traffic share. Click a row to filter Recent
  evaluations to that version.

**Run judge now** grades recent classified reports straight away. It spends AI budget.
Otherwise the judge runs nightly.

---

## Prompt promotion

You edit and A/B test prompts in [AI prompts](/admin/prompt-lab). The nightly judge
run promotes a candidate prompt on its own once it has at least 30 evaluations and its
mean score is more than 5% above the active prompt's.

---

## Drift alerts

When this week's mean score is more than 10% below last week's, the nightly judge run
records a drift alert and, if the project has a Slack webhook set, posts it to Slack.
The **Week change** figure at the top shows the same comparison.
