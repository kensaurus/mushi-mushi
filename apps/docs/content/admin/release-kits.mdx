# Release kits

**Where:** Releases → open a release → **Ship it** → Release kit

A release kit holds what a product video and fresh store media need for one release:

- **Before and after screenshots** of the screens that changed, copied from your UX runs
  so they are kept with the release.
- **Clips** of your top flows (web apps, recorded with `mushi-ux clips`), or **device
  recordings** you upload (native apps).
- **Changelog lines** from the release notes, and your **brand** (name, accent colour,
  theme) from the widget settings. Both are editable.
- A **preset**: App Store preview, Google Play promo, Social 9:16 or Social 1:1. Each
  asset shows inline whether it fits the preset's spec. Image sizes are read from the
  file; a video's length, frame rate and codec are shown as declared.

Mushi Mushi never renders video.

---

## Record clips (opt-in)

Turn on **Record clips for release kits** in the kit, then:

```bash
mushi-ux clips --url https://staging.example.com --release <release id> \
  --flow "signup:/,/signup" --flow "pricing:/pricing"
```

Clips are capped per app (30 seconds and 25 MB by default). A clip is WebM source footage
for the video tool, not something a store accepts as is.

## Make the product video

**Export for a product video** (or the MCP tool `export_release_kit`) returns a `kitUrl`
valid for 24 hours, in lazycow's release-kit format (every file with its own signed link),
and the lazycow preset to use (`appstore-iphone`, `play-promo`, `social-9x16` or
`social-1x1`). App Store previews show only full-screen captures of the app, so the
"after" screens and recordings matter there; "before" screens feed the social and Play
cuts. Hand both to lazycow:

```text
make_product_video({ kitUrl, preset })
```

lazycow renders on your machine and sends the file back to the kit. The contract is in
[`docs/integrations/lazycow-release-kit.md`](https://github.com/kensaurus/mushi-mushi/blob/master/docs/integrations/lazycow-release-kit.md).

## Put media on the stores (approval first)

With App Store Connect or Google Play connected and **Act** turned on for it (with a
separate write key), the kit's **Store uploads** card asks for:

- App Store: screenshots for one device size, or app previews (15–30 s, H.264 or ProRes
  422 HQ, at most 30 fps).
- Google Play: listing images (phone screenshots, feature graphic, icon), or the promo
  video as a YouTube link.

Asking does not upload anything. An owner or admin approves the exact request (every file
is pinned by its hash), then runs it once within the hour. Agents can ask with
`request_store_media_upload`; only a person in the console can approve. Listing text
still goes through your repo and your CI.
