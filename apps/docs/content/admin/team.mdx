# Team

**Route:** `/team` (sidebar: **Team**)

> **Scenario:** You want to invite someone, check the bill, see what AI calls cost,
> or change how your team signs in, without hunting through the sidebar.

Everything about your team is one page. Pick a view at the top; the link keeps it
(`/team?view=billing`), so you can bookmark or share it.

| View | What it is for | Where it used to be |
|------|----------------|---------------------|
| [**Members**](/admin/teams) | Invite teammates and set roles. Plans with teams (Studio, Enterprise). | `/organization/members` |
| [**Billing**](/admin/billing) | Plan, invoices and the spend cap. | `/billing` |
| [**AI spend**](/admin/cost) | What diagnoses and fixes cost, by project and model. | `/cost` |
| [**Audit log**](/admin/audit) | Who changed what, when. Plans with the audit log. | `/audit` |
| [**Single sign-on**](/admin/sso) | SAML / OIDC for your team. Plans with SSO. | `/sso` |
| [**Compliance**](/admin/compliance) | SOC 2 evidence, data requests. Plans with SOC 2. | `/compliance` |
| [**Storage**](/admin/storage) | Where screenshots and attachments are kept. | `/storage` |

Views your plan does not include are hidden. Old links still work: each former route
opens its view here with the rest of the link kept.

Project settings (keys, SDK, integrations for one app) stay on [Settings](/admin/settings).
