# Your phone app (Expo, React Native or Capacitor)

On a phone, people rarely email you when something breaks. They close the app.
Mushi Mushi gives them a quick way to tell you instead, and tells you why it
broke in plain English.

## What your users do

- They **shake the phone**, or tap a small bug button, and type one sentence
  about what went wrong.
- The report can include a **screenshot** of the screen they were on.
- No signal? The report **waits on the phone** and sends itself when the phone
  is back online.

## What you get

Each report lands in your Mushi Mushi inbox with a short note: what broke, how
serious it is, the likely cause, and a **fix prompt** you can paste into
Cursor, Claude Code or another AI editor. [See a sample](/demo/diagnosis).

Both install from npm. The fastest way is to run `npx mushi-mushi` in your
app's folder: it finds your setup, installs the right package and saves your
project ID and key. On Expo and React Native, shake-to-report and screenshots
each use one small extra package, which the setup page lists. On Capacitor
they are settings. The setup takes a few minutes, then you make a new build of
your app so the button ships with it.

The free plan covers one app and 100 diagnoses a month, with no card and no AI
key of your own. See [pricing](/pricing).
